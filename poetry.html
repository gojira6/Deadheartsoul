Love · English

All of Me

Not the polite little smile.
The proper one.
The one that reaches your eyes.
The one I'll know
because I put it there.

Then I'll hold out my hands,
pull you back up,
dust you down,
check you're okay.

To catch you when you stumble.
To steady you when you sway.
And when the road gets rough,
to keep walking.

Beside you.

I promise never to hide my true colours from you.

The loud ones.
The weird ones.
The ridiculous ones.
The ones that clash spectacularly
and really shouldn't work together.

I promise you'll get all of me.

The serious me.
The silly me.
The stubborn me.
The soft me.
The "I'm definitely right" me—

Because amongst all the colours,
all the chaos,
all the laughter,
all the arguments,
all the weirdness…

somehow,

you and me
just make sense.
— Suhail Anjum

Bol, desh ko desh samjho,
Bol, izzat kamayi hui teri hai.

Bol, mujhe tu insaaf dega,
Bol, tere haathon mein insaaf ki ghadi hai.

Bol, ke ye thoda waqt bahut hai,
Bol, ye marne se pehle…

Bol, ke lab azaad hain tere,
Bol, zubaan ab tak teri hai.
Bol, ke jaan ab tak teri hai.
— Suhail Anjum

Love · English

Before Time

I often question the logic of fate and destiny, but there is no part of my love for you that could not have been written before time was even half a thought.

Before we were people you could hold and be held by, my atoms knew and loved yours.

When we were stardust on the lips of something before breath, we kissed with each word that spoke us into existence.

If there is no string of fate that connects us, I will craft one twined with my heartstrings and vocal cords, so that I may love you loudly.

I will loop it around our hands and hearts, then double-knot it for good measure.

If you were not made for me, then I will make myself to be made yours.

If I choose to be nothing else, I choose to be yours.
— Suhail Anjum

008
Ishq · Long Form

Ruka Hua Ishq

Dil rukta hi hai na,
Jahan lag jaaye, udhar toh rukta hi hai.

Mere khayal, mere jazbaat,
Bas wahi ruke hue hain jahan
dil ruka hua hai.

Tere paas hi rakha hai,
Ijaazat dena tera hi kaam hai.
Magar ye ishq hai na…
bas ruka hua hai.

Tera jaana aasaan lagta hi hoga tujhe,
Mere bina waqt bitana bhi aasaan laga hoga.

Par woh main nahi tha jisne teri rooh kabze mein karke rakhi thi,
Woh tu thi jisne mera kabza karke rakha tha.

Tu chahe aage badh jaaye,
Tu chahe dil badal le,
Tu rakh le jo tujhe rakhna ho is rishte se.

Magar
main…

Main woh rakhunga
jiska wada tujhse kar gaya tha.

Bata kar hi jaunga tujhse
mohabbat ka hashr kya tha.

Woh ab jo na-mumkin sa ho gaya hai,
woh ab jo zid mein har din sa ho gaya hai,
ishq ibaadat hai — inhe samajh thodi aani hai.

Main likhunga ab tak
ki jo teri meri kahani hai.

Woh dhaaga jo utaar degi na tu,
woh dhaaga jo utaar dunga main—
woh din aakhri ho mera,
woh rasm aakhri ho teri.

Ismein jo reh jaaye zinda ashaar dimaag mein,
bas utna haq bacha hua hai.

Ye ishq hai na…
bas utna ruka hua hai.
— Suhail Anjum

010
Punjabi · Pain

Kede Rang

Kade kade hun sochan lag jaavan,
Kehde rang vich rang layi ae duniya main apni.

Te kujh karna chaahuna, te kujh kar vi na paavaan,
Kehde tanj vich kass layi ae duniya main apni.

Koi haal puchhe na, te koi gale laun nu agge na aave,
Mainu lukko ke pachhtaan reh giya soorataan,
hun ohnu koi matthe na laave.

Nazraan vekhan bhull gayiaan ne,
Nazraan nu vekh ke dard chirh aunda.
Ohde kisse yaad kar-kar ke ve,
ankhiyan ton hanju dig aunda.

Keeda aitbaar karaan?
Te keenu chhad tur javaan?

Mainu oss di sauh khawaan waleyo,
main keedi sauh khaawan?
English Translation

Sometimes I sit and wonder,
in what colours has this world painted me?

I want to do something,
yet sometimes I cannot do anything at all.
In what cruelty has this world tied me down?

No one asks how I am,
and no one comes forward to embrace me.

My eyes have forgotten how to look,
because looking itself brings pain.
Remembering their stories,
tears fall from my eyes.

Whom should I trust?
And whom should I leave and walk away from?

You who swear by their name,
tell me — whose oath am I supposed to take?

— Suhail Anjum
