The story behind the soul

It started in 2018.

Not with a plan. Not with a dream of becoming a writer.

It started when life became difficult enough that words were easier to write than to speak.

Things at home weren't right. Things inside me weren't right either. There were problems I didn't know how to solve, feelings I didn't know how to explain, and a kind of helplessness that slowly teaches you to stay quiet.

I wasn't the kind of person who could sit down and tell someone,

“I'm not okay.”

Not even the people closest to me.

So I found another way.

I started writing.

At first, I wasn't good at it. But I kept going.

Because when you can't say something to another person, sometimes a blank page becomes the only place where you can finally be honest.

One page became another. One thought became another. And somewhere between the words, I started leaving pieces of myself behind.

Years passed.

Around 500 posts later, I realised something.

I wasn't just writing anymore.

I had built a place where everything I couldn't say had a voice.

And that place became DeadHeart Soul.

Why this name?

Why a Dead Heart?

There was a time when I felt like I had seen and felt enough to become numb.

Not sad.
Not angry.
Just... empty.

And when you feel nothing for long enough, it starts feeling like something inside you has died.

So I gave that feeling a name.

DeadHeart Soul.

A soul that feels dead — but has seen enough life to remember everything.
